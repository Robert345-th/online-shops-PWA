Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

ZedMarket ("we", "our", "us") operates the ZedMarket marketplace app and website at zedmarket.app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, password (stored securely), and optional profile or shop information you provide.
  • Listings and messages: photos, descriptions, prices, locations, and chat content you post or send in the app.
  • Location: approximate location if you choose to share it for listings, maps, or meetups.
  • Device and usage: basic technical data such as browser type, app install status, and push notification tokens.
  • Payments: subscription or boost requests may include transaction references you submit for admin review. We do not process card payments inside the app.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and shop.
  • To show listings, chats, favorites, and notifications.
  • To review shop applications, reports, and payment requests.
  • To improve safety, prevent abuse, and enforce our rules.
  • To send SMS verification codes and important service messages.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Other users, when you post listings, chat, or share a shop profile.
  • Service providers that help us host the app, send SMS, store images, or deliver push notifications.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users from fraud or harm.

Your choices

  • You can update profile, shop, and location settings in the app.
  • You can turn off online status, last seen, or push notifications in Settings.
  • You can request account deletion from Settings. We will remove or anonymize your data where reasonably possible.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong password and be careful when sharing phone numbers or meeting buyers and sellers.

Children

ZedMarket is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of ZedMarket after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through in-app Customer Support or email the ZedMarket team via the contact details shown in the app.
