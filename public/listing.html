Before you contact the seller
Do not pay until you see the item.
Never send mobile money or any payment before you meet the seller and check the item in person. If someone pushes you to pay first, stop and walk away.
Meeting Safely
•Meet in a public place, like a mall, police station, or busy shopping area.
•Never send payment before seeing and checking the item in person.
•Bring a friend or family member with you if possible.
•Tell someone where you're going using "Share My Meetup" below.
•If something feels wrong, trust your instinct and walk away.
